Sensible repairs for the appliances you rely on every day

When an appliance fails, you need an honest assessment of what is wrong and what it will cost to put right. We focus on electrical repairs across four core kitchen appliances: washing machines, electric cookers, dishwashers and refrigeration.

What we repair

Washing machines & dryers

Diagnosis and replacement of failed drain pumps, drum bearings, worn carbon brushes, leaking door seals and drive belts on freestanding and integrated units.

  • Drainage and spinning failures
  • Snagged or torn door seals
  • Error codes and motor faults

Electric ovens & cookers

Fault-finding for electric fan ovens, elements and thermostats that cause uneven heating, cold spots or tripped circuits.

  • Blown fan oven elements
  • Thermostat calibration
  • Door hinges and inner glass repairs

Dishwashers

Resolving drainage blockages, faulty inlet valves, worn spray arms and heating problems so dishes come out clean and dry.

  • Standing water in the base
  • Heater element and sensor replacement
  • Dispenser and spray arm repairs

Fridges & freezers

Tackling iced-up evaporator fans, failing defrost timers, thermostat faults and damaged magnetic door gaskets.

  • Over-cooling or warm interiors
  • Excess frost build-up
  • Defective door seals and sensors

How booking works

Three steps from your first call to a finished repair.

  1. 1

    Initial conversation

    Call with your appliance brand, model number and a short description of the fault. We’ll confirm whether an in-person diagnostic makes financial sense.

  2. 2

    Diagnostic and clear quote

    We inspect the machine on site, identify the failed component and give you a written parts-and-labour total before any further work.

  3. 3

    Repair and functional test

    Once approved, we fit the part, test full operation, carry out basic earth testing and leave your kitchen tidy.

Our operating principles

We don’t publish invented reviews or badges. Our approach rests on four plain commitments.

Upfront diagnostic fees

You’re told our fixed diagnostic rate before we arrive. No surprise attendance fees or mileage surcharges.

Repair or replace, honestly

If an appliance is beyond economic repair, or the part costs more than a new machine, we say so directly.

Respect for your home

Protective floor mats, clean shoe covers and a tidy-up after every inspection or part replacement.

Electrical safety checks

Every domestic electrical repair ends with basic earth continuity and insulation testing before the unit returns to service.
